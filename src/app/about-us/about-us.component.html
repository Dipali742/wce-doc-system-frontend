WHY WCE E-DOC SYSTEM?

Legal document such as Passport, Birth certificate etc. Are very important and it can be misused. Therefore we have developed a system that saves the document in an encrypted form and stores it in the internal memory. The main advantage of this system is that it asks for Secure pin to access the file.
